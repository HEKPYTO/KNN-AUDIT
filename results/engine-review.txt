Engine spot-check — 2026-11-21

Reviewer: independent agent, not the adapter author.
Method: five failing and two passing cases per engine were queried directly
against Qdrant REST and pgvector SQL, bypassing the Rust adapters. The adapter
answers were compared with the direct answers at the same limit and with the
exact ground-truth ids.

Results:
- Qdrant: 5/5 failing answers reproduced byte-identically outside the adapter;
  2/2 passing answers equal the ground-truth set.
- pgvector: 5/5 failing answers reproduced byte-identically outside the
  adapter; 2/2 passing answers equal the ground-truth set.
- Sampled mismatches are exact ties at ranks 10 and 11 under the engine's own
  scores (for example two equal scores of 13393.025) while exact arithmetic
  separates the distances.
- Qdrant's tie pick changes with the requested limit, confirming arbitrary tie
  order rather than an adapter parsing bug.

Verdict: no adapter bug explains the reported mismatches.
